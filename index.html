THE PROBLEM

Most schools and facilities already have security cameras — but footage alone isn't enough.

Security teams need faster situational awareness without replacing trusted camera systems or adding invasive technology.

01

Reactive review

Footage is often checked after an incident, leaving teams without real-time context when it matters most.

02

Missed context

Live camera feeds can be difficult to monitor continuously, especially across large campuses with multiple entry points.

03

Costly hardware cycles

New sensor networks can be expensive, disruptive, and slow to deploy when the cameras you need are already installed.

04

False alarm fatigue

Alerts need human judgment so teams can act quickly without triggering unnecessary responses that drain attention and trust.
